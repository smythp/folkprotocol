## Summary

The draft has significantly improved from the original in many places, but substantial source-method narration remains throughout. Phrases like "one trial tested," "in one study," "a review found," "indexed snippets describe," and "the accessible tertiary source yielded" still frame the prose as reporting on sources rather than stating what happens or what is known. Several concrete improvements are needed:

1. Replace remaining "one trial tested / one study found / a review found" with direct statements of what happens or what is known
2. Translate the one remaining scale score (line 64) into plain language
3. Remove or compress the project meta-commentary at lines 55-56, 98, 100, 105, and 106
4. Preserve the concrete outcome descriptions, direct warnings, and clean distinctions that are already working well

The voice should sound like someone who looked through the territory and can state what's known without showing the research scaffolding.
