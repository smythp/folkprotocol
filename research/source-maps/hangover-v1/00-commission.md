# Commission

## Slug

hangover-v1

## Question

What practices might reduce the likelihood or severity of an alcohol hangover, or make recovery easier?

## Boundaries

Adult occasional drinking; before/during and next-day; excludes withdrawal, alcohol use disorder treatment, emergency care, and generic moralizing.

## Seeds supplied by the commissioner

Existing Folkprotocol hangover page and bibliography are prior material, not authority.

The commissioner is interested in practices whose effects could become
noticeable through ordinary use over time, not a quantified-self regimen,
fixed drinking challenges, spreadsheets, or symptom scoring. Personal
observations offered as leads rather than established findings include:

- drinking earlier has sometimes produced markedly better next-day results;
- a substantial whole-milk meal has strongly changed the felt alcohol
  trajectory, especially when younger;
- IPAs tend to produce a worse headache and sometimes more gastrointestinal
  symptoms than other drinks;
- outdoor swimming can produce a large next-day improvement, plausibly through
  exertion, circulation, temperature, light, breathing, or arousal rather than
  "sweating out toxins";
- sleep loss, gastrointestinal symptoms, headache, and low-motivation or
  physically inert states may be partly distinct paths rather than one uniform
  hangover; and
- aspirin has historically helped headache, while Alka-Seltzer has been useful
  for gastrointestinal symptoms at a different point in the episode.

These observations should sharpen questions and preserve plausible directions;
they do not by themselves establish mechanisms or general effects.

## Working boundary

This commission covers practices an adult who drinks occasionally might use before, during, or the day after drinking, aimed at reducing the likelihood or severity of a hangover or easing recovery from one. It does not cover alcohol withdrawal, treatment for alcohol use disorder, or emergency medical care for acute alcohol poisoning or related crises. It is not a brief for moralizing about drinking in general. Any existing Folkprotocol page or bibliography on this topic is prior material to be examined, not a starting authority or a fixed scope to preserve.
