# Tone review: hemorrhoids-v1

## Factual fidelity issues

### Opening paragraph: Causation claim shifted (line 10)

**Factual draft:** "although it does not show which path caused any one person's symptoms"

**Tone draft:** "though the paths are not mutually exclusive and a person's case might involve more than one"

The original says we cannot determine which specific path caused an individual's symptoms. The rewrite says multiple paths can apply to one person. These are related but different claims. The original is about diagnostic uncertainty; the rewrite is about co-occurrence.

**Repair:** Restore the original meaning. "This makes a pressure-driven episode without constipation plausible, though it does not show which path caused any one person's symptoms."

### MPFF pregnancy data: Qualification removed (line 90)

**Factual draft:** "human data are absent or limited"

**Tone draft:** "human data are limited"

The rewrite dropped "absent or" from the product information's pregnancy warning. This narrows the stated data gap.

**Repair:** Restore the complete qualification: "because human data are absent or limited"

### Rutosides causal link: Phrasing changed meaning (line 94)

**Factual draft:** "without an established causal link"

**Tone draft:** "and the study did not establish a causal link"

The original phrasing is neutral about whether the study attempted to establish causation. The rewrite implies the study tried and failed. For adverse events in small samples, "without an established causal link" is the appropriate framing.

**Repair:** Use the original: "without an established causal link"
