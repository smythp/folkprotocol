# Content audit

## Proposed mechanisms are stated as established actions

`120-tone-draft.md` strengthens three mechanistic accounts beyond the upstream material:

- Line 10 removes the attribution to a clinical review and states the pregnancy-pressure paths directly. `90-protocol-dossiers.md` explicitly calls these the review authors' explanation and says the account makes a non-constipation route plausible.
- Line 16 says oral phlebotonics "act systemically on venous tone, capillary permeability, or lymphatic drainage." The dossier and `100-factual-draft.md` describe these as proposed class actions and say the precise mechanism is not established. The appended caveat does not undo the preceding factual verb.
- Line 32 says the physiological study "found a thermosphincteric reflex." The dossier and factual draft say the measured pressure and electrical changes led the authors to propose that reflex as an explanation.

Restore attribution or proposal language in all three places so the observed findings remain distinct from their mechanistic interpretations.

## The MPFF formulation is made more specific than the dossier supports

Line 90 says Daflon 500 contains "450 mg diosmin and 50 mg hesperidin per tablet." The dossier and factual draft give the product specification as 450 mg diosmin plus 50 mg flavonoids expressed as hesperidin. Those formulations are not interchangeable: the tone pass turns the expression basis into a claim that the entire 50 mg is hesperidin. Restore the dossier's exact composition language.
