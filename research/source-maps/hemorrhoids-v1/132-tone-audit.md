# Independent tone audit: hemorrhoids-v1 tone draft

## Line 10: Qualification swamps observation

**"though it does not show which path caused any one person's symptoms"**

The brief instructed: cut this or say that paths are not mutually exclusive and a person's case might involve more than one. The preceding sentence already established that the paths can coexist. This qualifier adds no decision and swamps the observation.

**Repair:** Either cut entirely, or rewrite as: "A person's case may involve more than one path."

## Line 56: Report voice transition

**"The following schedules are product-specific and do not transfer to other strengths, combinations, or routes."**

"The following" is assistant/report voice — narrating what comes next rather than stating the point directly.

**Repair:** "These schedules are product-specific and do not transfer to other strengths, combinations, or routes."

## Lines 58-66: Laundry-list structure

The five-bullet active list follows identical structure for each item: active, labeled use, frequency, boundary. The repetition makes the list skim-proof. The brief suggested either breaking into two passes (actives and their jobs, then schedules) or adding a lead sentence about actives doing different jobs and the choice following the symptom.

The draft added "The choice follows the symptom" at line 56, but this comes before explaining what the different jobs are. The bullets still present all five actives with identical treatment regardless of whether that detail changes the decision.

**Repair:** Add a sentence before the bullets that names the different jobs: "Anesthetics target pain, vasoconstrictors target swelling, and steroids target itch." Or restructure into two passes.

## Line 94: Evidence-status tic

**"without an established causal link"**

The brief flagged this as evidence-status tic and suggested "without a clear causal link" or "and the study did not establish a causal link."

**Repair:** "without a clear causal link"

## Line 102: Report voice summary

**"The time windows for ordinary use are day-four relief with MPFF (uncontrolled pregnancy report), two to four weeks with rutosides (controlled trial), and two weeks with oral-plus-topical Euphorbia."**

The brief said this is report voice cataloging what was already stated, and if it stays it should be rewritten as direct advice rather than a summary of what was reported.

**Repair:** Either cut as redundant, or rewrite as: "MPFF showed day-four relief in an uncontrolled pregnancy report, rutosides showed change over two to four weeks in a controlled trial, and Euphorbia with cream showed change after two weeks."
