# Fenugreek (Trigonella foenum-graecum) — Clinical Uses

## Testosterone & Hormonal Effects

### Mansoori 2020 — Meta-Analysis of Testosterone Effects
- **Design:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs
- **Scope:** 4 RCTs, 199 total participants
- **Primary finding:** Fenugreek extract significantly increased total testosterone
  - Weighted mean difference (WMD): **0.89 nmol/L** (95% CI: 0.12–1.66, p = 0.02)
- **No significant effect** on free testosterone or estradiol
- Subgroup analyses by intervention duration and health status were non-significant, likely due to the small number of included studies
- **Assessment:** Modest effect. 0.89 nmol/L is a real but small increase — context matters relative to baseline levels. Authors note more high-quality RCTs are needed.
- PMID: 32048383

### Rao 2016 — Testofen Trial (Sexual Function + Testosterone)
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- **Sample:** n=120, healthy aging males aged 43–70 years
- **Dosing:** Testofen (standardized fenugreek extract), 600 mg/day for 12 weeks
- **Results:**
  - Both total and free testosterone increased significantly vs placebo
  - Sexual function improved significantly: number of morning erections (p<0.001), frequency of sexual activity (p<0.001)
  - Serum prolactin and estradiol were unchanged
  - Effective at reducing symptoms of androgen decline (ADAM questionnaire)
- **Assessment:** Well-designed RCT with adequate sample size. The dual finding of hormonal + functional improvement is notable. Testofen is a proprietary standardized extract — results may not generalize to all fenugreek products.
- PMID: 26791805

### Wankhede 2016 — Body Composition in Resistance-Trained Males
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot study
- **Sample:** n=60, healthy resistance-trained male subjects
- **Dosing:** Fenugreek extract, 600 mg/day for 8 weeks
- **Results:**
  - Significant decrease in body fat (p=0.01)
  - Significant increase in lean mass (p=0.004)
  - Leg press performance improved significantly (p=0.007)
  - Serum total testosterone increased in fenugreek group, but **the change was NOT statistically significant** vs placebo
- **Assessment:** Interesting body composition changes without reaching significance on testosterone. Described as a "pilot study" — n=60 may be underpowered for hormone endpoints. Suggests fenugreek's effects on body comp may operate through mechanisms beyond direct testosterone elevation.
- PMID: 30356905

### Maheshwari 2017 — Furosap Open-Label Study
- **Design:** Open-label, single-arm, longitudinal study (NO placebo control, NO blinding)
- **Sample:** n=100, healthy male volunteers aged 35–60 years
- **Dosing:** Furosap (standardized to 20% protodioscin), 500 mg/day for 12 weeks
- **Results:**
  - Free testosterone reportedly improved in up to **90%** of participants
  - Total testosterone improved in **46.8%** of volunteers
  - Sperm motility, morphology, and count improved significantly (p<0.001)
  - Mental alertness, mood, and libido improved significantly
  - No significant adverse events
- **Study quality note:** This is an **open-label, single-arm study** — the weakest design among the trials reviewed here. No placebo group means the 90% improvement claim cannot be disentangled from placebo effect, regression to the mean, or other confounders. The percentage-of-participants-improved framing (rather than mean change with CI) further obscures the data. Treat these numbers with appropriate skepticism.
- PMID: 28280480 / PMC5340133

## Women's Libido & Sexual Function

### Rao 2015 — Libifem Trial in Women
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- **Sample:** n=80, healthy menstruating women with self-reported low sexual drive
- **Dosing:** Libifem (standardized fenugreek extract), 600 mg/day for 2 menstrual cycles
- **Results:**
  - Significant increase in free testosterone and estradiol vs placebo (p<0.05)
  - Significant increase in sexual desire and arousal vs placebo (p<0.05)
  - No adverse effects reported
- **Assessment:** One of the few fenugreek trials in women. Good design, adequate sample. Libifem is another proprietary extract from the same research group (Rao/Steels/Vitetta).
- PMID: 25914334

## Muscle Performance

### Albaker 2023 — Systematic Review of Muscle Performance
- **Design:** Systematic review of RCTs
- **Scope:** 6 RCTs
- **Results:**
  - 4 of 6 RCTs showed significant improvements in muscle strength, endurance, lean body mass, and reduced body fat
  - Fenugreek glycoside supplementation demonstrated substantial anabolic and androgenic activity
  - One study showed improved glycogen resynthesis post-exercise
  - One study noted improved creatine uptake when co-administered with fenugreek
- PMID: 37233192 / PMC10219128

## Blood Sugar & Glycemic Control

### Neelakantan 2014 — Meta-Analysis of Glycemic Effects
- **Design:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs
- **Scope:** 10 RCTs
- **Results:**
  - Fasting blood glucose reduced by **-0.96 mmol/L** vs control
  - 2-hour postload glucose reduced by **-2.19 mmol/L** vs control
  - HbA1c reduced by **-0.85%** vs control
- **Assessment:** Clinically meaningful reductions across all glycemic markers. The blood sugar effects are among the best-supported clinical applications of fenugreek.
- PMID: 24840284

## Galactagogue (Breast Milk Production)

### Khan 2018 — Milk Production and Newborn Weight
- **Design:** Randomized clinical trial
- **Sample:** n=78
- **Results:**
  - Fenugreek group had significantly higher breast milk production on day 3 and day 7
  - Newborns in fenugreek group had less weight loss and regained birth weight faster
- **Assessment:** Supports the longstanding traditional use as a galactagogue. Note the pregnancy contraindication discussed in safety — this use is postpartum only.
- PMID: 29720942
