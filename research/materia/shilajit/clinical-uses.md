# Shilajit - Clinical Uses

## Evidence-Based Clinical Applications

### 1. Testosterone Enhancement (Healthy Men)

**Study**: Pandit et al. 2016 (Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial)
- **Sample**: 75 healthy male volunteers, aged 45-55 years
- **Dosage**: 250 mg purified Shilajit (PrimaVie) twice daily (500 mg/day total)
- **Duration**: 90 days
- **Results**:
  - Total testosterone increased by 20.45% (p < 0.05)
  - Free testosterone increased by 19.14% (p < 0.05)
  - DHEA (Dehydroepiandrosterone) increased by 31.35% (p < 0.05)
  - Luteinizing Hormone (LH) and Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) levels were well-maintained
- **PMID**: 26395129
- **DOI**: 10.1111/and.12482
- **Journal**: Andrologia 48(5):570-575

**Notable**: This study is significant because it demonstrates testosterone-raising effects in *healthy* men — not just those with deficiency or infertility. Many adaptogens (e.g., tongkat ali) primarily show testosterone effects in stressed, deficient, or infertile populations. Pandit 2016 shows shilajit works even when the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis is functioning normally, which is a stronger claim. The DHEA increase (+31.35%) is particularly striking as it suggests upstream adrenal support rather than just gonadal stimulation.

### 2. Male Fertility (Infertile Men)

**Study**: Biswas et al. 2010 (Clinical trial)
- **Sample**: 60 infertile males with oligospermia (sperm count <20 million/mL)
- **Population**: Males aged 25-40 with oligozoospermia
- **Dosage**: 100 mg processed Shilajit capsules twice daily (200 mg/day total)
- **Duration**: 90 days
- **Results**:
  - Total sperm count increased by 61.4% (p < 0.001)
  - Sperm motility improved by 12.4% to 17.4% (p < 0.01)
  - Normal sperm morphology increased by 18.9% (p < 0.001)
  - Serum testosterone increased by 23.5% (p < 0.001)
  - FSH levels decreased significantly (p < 0.01), indicating reduced testicular stress
  - Malondialdehyde (MDA, marker of oxidative stress) decreased in seminal plasma by 18.7% (p < 0.001)
- **PMID**: 20078516
- **DOI**: 10.1111/j.1439-0272.2009.00956.x
- **Journal**: Andrologia 42(1):48-56

**Notable**: The 61.4% increase in total sperm count is a very large effect size. The simultaneous decrease in MDA (-18.7%) suggests the mechanism involves reducing oxidative damage to spermatozoa. Lower dose than Pandit (200 mg vs 500 mg) but still effective — though this was an infertile population where there may be more room for improvement.

### 3. Exercise Performance and Muscle Strength

**Study**: Keller et al. 2019 (Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial)
- **Sample**: 63 recreationally active adults
- **Dosage**: 250 mg/day or 500 mg/day purified Shilajit (PrimaVie)
- **Duration**: 8 weeks
- **Results**:
  - 500 mg/day maintained maximal voluntary isometric contraction (MVIC) strength better than placebo following a fatiguing protocol (p = 0.024)
  - 500 mg/day resulted in lower serum hydroxyproline concentration compared to placebo (p = 0.038), suggesting reduced collagen degradation
  - **250 mg/day did NOT show significant differences from placebo**
- **PMID**: 30728074
- **PMC**: PMC6364418
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12970-019-0270-2
- **Journal**: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition 16(1):3

**Notable**: This is a dose-response study. The clear threshold effect — 500 mg effective, 250 mg not — establishes a minimum effective dose for exercise-related outcomes. The reduced hydroxyproline suggests shilajit may protect connective tissue during intense exercise.

### 4. Cognitive Function and Neuroprotection

**Study**: Carrasco-Gallardo et al. 2012 (Review, focus on mechanism)
- **Key Findings**:
  - Fulvic acid inhibits aggregation of tau protein fibrils in vitro — tau aggregation is a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease
  - Fulvic acid can disassemble pre-formed tau fibrils, suggesting potential therapeutic (not just preventive) role
  - Shilajit's antioxidant properties and mineral delivery to target tissues contribute to neuroprotective effects
  - Authors describe shilajit as "a potent and very safe dietary supplement, potentially able to prevent several diseases, but its main medical application now appears to come from its actions in benefit of cognitive processes"
- **PMID**: 22482077
- **PMC**: PMC3296184
- **DOI**: 10.1155/2012/674142
- **Journal**: International Journal of Alzheimer's Disease 2012:674142

**Notable**: This is a review/mechanism paper, not an RCT. The tau fibril disassembly finding is from in vitro work. No human clinical trials on cognition yet — this remains a promising but unproven application.

## Summary of Dosing Across Trials

| Study | Population | Daily Dose | Duration | Primary Outcome |
|-------|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Pandit 2016 | Healthy men 45-55 | 500 mg (PrimaVie) | 90 days | +20.45% total T, +19.14% free T, +31.35% DHEA |
| Biswas 2010 | Infertile men 25-40 | 200 mg (processed) | 90 days | +23.5% T, +61.4% sperm count |
| Keller 2019 | Active adults | 500 mg (PrimaVie) | 8 weeks | Maintained MVIC strength post-fatigue |
| Keller 2019 | Active adults | 250 mg (PrimaVie) | 8 weeks | No significant effect |

## Evidence Quality Assessment

The testosterone/fertility evidence is based on only 2 clinical trials (Pandit and Biswas), both from overlapping research groups. While results are consistent and effect sizes are notable, replication by independent groups would strengthen confidence. The exercise study (Keller) comes from an independent US lab, which is encouraging. The cognitive evidence is mechanistic only — no human trials.
