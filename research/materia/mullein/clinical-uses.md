# Mullein — Clinical Uses

*Verbascum thapsus L. | Evidence as of 2026-02-28*

---

## Regulatory Approval

**EMA/HMPC Traditional Use Registration (2018)**
- Indication: Relief of sore throat associated with dry cough and cold
- Population: Adults and adolescents ≥ 12 years
- Basis: Article 16d Directive 2001/83/EC — at least 30 years documented traditional use, minimum 15 years within EU; no clinical trial requirement
- Duration: Maximum 1 week without medical supervision
- Reference: EMA/HMPC/611537/2016

## Randomized Controlled Trials

### Episiotomy Wound Healing (Topical)
- **Citation:** Taleb & Saeedi 2021 (PMID 34103042), *BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies*
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **N:** 93 primiparous women with episiotomy wounds
- **Intervention:** Topical V. thapsus cream, 2 cm applied twice daily × 10 days
- **Comparator:** Matched placebo cream
- **Outcome:** REEDA scale (Redness, Edema, Ecchymosis, Discharge, Approximation)
- **Results:**
  - Days 1 and 3: No significant difference between groups
  - Day 10: REEDA scores significantly better in V. thapsus group vs. placebo (p = 0.01)
- **Applicability:** Only standalone V. thapsus RCT; topical route only; wound-healing endpoint — not generalizable to oral respiratory indications

### Ear Pain / Acute Otitis Media — 2001 RCT (Multi-herb)
- **Citation:** Sarrell, Mandelberg, Cohen 2001 (PMID 11434846), *Archives of Pediatrics & Adolescent Medicine*
- **Design:** Randomized controlled trial
- **N:** 103 children aged 6–18 years with ear pain and acute otitis media
- **Intervention:** Otikon Otic Solution ear drops (Allium sativum + V. thapsus + Calendula flores + Hypericum perforatum in olive oil)
- **Comparator:** Anaesthetic ear drops (amethocaine, phenazone, glycerine)
- **Outcome:** Ear pain VAS over 3 days
- **Results:**
  - Both groups: statistically significant pain improvement (p = 0.007)
  - Naturopathic extracts performed at least as well as anaesthetic drops
  - ~80% of pain was self-limited
- **Limitations:** Mullein is 1 of 4 active components; individual contribution indeterminate; no placebo-only arm

### Ear Pain / Acute Otitis Media — 2003 RCT (Multi-herb, Expanded)
- **Citation:** Sarrell, Cohen, Kahan 2003 (PMID 12728112), *Pediatrics*
- **Design:** Double-blind randomized controlled trial
- **N:** 171 children aged 5–18 years with ear pain
- **Intervention:** NHED ear drops (Allium sativum + V. thapsus + Calendula flores + Hypericum perforatum + lavender + vitamin E in olive oil); 5 drops TID
- **Comparator:** Topical anesthetic alone; amoxicillin ± topical anesthetic; combinations
- **Dosing:** 5 drops instilled in affected ear, 3× daily
- **Results:**
  - All groups showed significant pain improvement over 3 days
  - NHED outperformed control in pain reduction
  - Most effective combination (NHED + topical anesthetic) explained only 7.3% of total pain reduction
  - Amoxicillin not contributory to pain outcomes
  - 80% of pain self-limited
- **Limitations:** Mullein 1 of 6 botanical components; high self-limitation rate; 3-day follow-up

## Evidence Gaps

- **No standalone RCTs for respiratory indications** — no clinical trials of V. thapsus alone for cough, expectorant, or bronchitis
- **No dose-ranging clinical studies** establishing minimum effective or maximum tolerated human doses for any route
- All positive respiratory-use evidence is traditional/ethnobotanical or preclinical; mechanism is plausible but clinically unvalidated
- No pharmacokinetic studies in humans
