# Tribulus terrestris - Preparations and Dosing

## Overview

Clinical trial evidence for Tribulus terrestris is almost entirely based on standardized dry extracts of the fruit, typically standardized to furostanol saponin (protodioscin) content. Traditional preparations (decoctions, granules, water extracts) differ substantially from pharmaceutical-grade extracts in bioactive content and predictability. One case of multi-organ toxicity was directly attributed to a traditional water extract.

---

## Pharmaceutical-Grade Standardized Extracts

### Tribestan® (Sopharma, Bulgaria) — Best Characterized

**Preparation type**: Film-coated tablets; dry fruit extract

**Tablet specification**:
- 250 mg TT fruit extract per tablet
- Standardized to ≥ 112.5 mg furostanol saponins per tablet (≥ 45% furostanol saponins)

**Clinical dosing protocol (Kamenov 2017)**:
- **Dose**: 6 tablets/day = 1,500 mg extract/day; ~675 mg furostanol saponins/day
- **Frequency**: 3 × 2 tablets after meals
- **Duration**: 12 weeks
- **Indication**: Mild-to-moderate erectile dysfunction (n=172; IIEF improvement p < 0.0001)

**Notes**:
- Most extensively studied preparation in controlled trials
- Taking after meals reduces GI adverse effects and may improve absorption
- Furostanol standardization ensures consistent protodioscin-equivalent content

---

### Androsten® — Fertility Applications

**Preparation type**: Capsules; dried TT extract

**Specification**:
- 250 mg dried extract per capsule
- Active compound: Protodioscin (standardized to protodioscin content)

**Studied dosing (Salgado 2017)**:
- **Population**: 65 men with abnormal semen evaluation
- **Findings**: Significant improvements in sperm concentration, motility, and liquefaction time; significant DHT increase; significant reduction in body fat; increase in lean mass

---

### Generic Standardized Extract Protocol (Fernández-Lázaro 2021)

**Specification**:
- 770 mg/day TT extract
- 72.64% active principles; 40% saponins (385 mg saponins/day)
- **Administration**: On empty stomach (fasted)

**Studied in**: CrossFit-trained males (n=30; 6 weeks)
- Testosterone maintenance during training; bench press improvement vs placebo

**Note**: Taking on empty stomach contrasts with the Tribestan® protocol (after meals) — the difference in GI tolerability and absorption with these two approaches is not directly compared in the literature

## Dosing by Indication (Evidence-Based Guidance)

### Erectile Dysfunction (Male)

**Recommended protocol (based on best evidence)**:
- **Dose**: 1,500 mg/day TT extract standardized to ≥ 40% furostanol saponins (equivalent: ~675 mg/day furostanol saponins)
- **Frequency**: Three divided doses after meals
- **Duration**: Minimum 8–12 weeks for efficacy assessment
- **Basis**: Kamenov 2017 (n=172; IIEF improvement 2.70 vs placebo, p < 0.0001); supported by Suharyani 2025 meta-analysis

**Lower-dose caution**: Santos 2014 (800 mg/day × 30 days) was negative; insufficient dose or duration may explain lack of effect

---

### Female Sexual Dysfunction — HSDD

**Postmenopausal women (De Souza 2016)**:
- **Dose**: 750 mg/day TT
- **Duration**: 120 days (4 months)
- **Outcomes**: Significant improvements in desire, arousal, pain, anorgasmia (FSFI and QS-F); free testosterone increased
- **Note**: Longer duration than most male ED trials; 4 months may be the appropriate minimum for this population

**Premenopausal women (Vale 2018)**:
- Dose protocol consistent with 250–750 mg range
- Total FSFI and domain-specific improvements (p < .001)

---

### Male Infertility / Oligozoospermia

**General guidance**:
- Use preparations standardized to protodioscin content (e.g., Androsten® or equivalent)
- Trial duration: Minimum 60–90 days (reflects spermatogenesis cycle of ~74 days)
- Sanagoo 2019 systematic review: 6 of 7 studies positive for sperm parameters at various doses

---

### Hypogonadism (Late-Onset, Confirmed Low Testosterone)

**GamalEl Din 2019 guidance**:
- 3× daily dosing, 3 months
- Testosterone effect restricted to hypogonadal subjects; do not use to raise testosterone in eugonadal men (no evidence of benefit, and confirmed negative in Neychev 2005)
- Monitor: AST, PSA, testosterone at baseline and 3 months

## Traditional Preparations

### Gokshura Granules (Ayurvedic)

**Preparation**: Dried fruit powder (choorna); may be made into granules with binders

**Typical dose in Ayurvedic practice**:
- 3–6 g dried fruit powder per day in two divided doses
- Mixed with warm water, milk, or honey
- **Duration**: 30–90 days typical for reproductive complaints

**Clinical study use**: Sellandi 2012 used Gokshura granules for 60 days in oligozoospermia; 78.11% improvement in clinical parameters (vs 70.95% placebo)

**Standardization**: None — protodioscin content entirely dependent on plant source and processing

---

### Decoctions (TCM Style)

**Preparation**: 5–15 g dried fruit in 300–500 mL water; simmer 20–30 minutes; strain

**Typical TCM dose**: 6–15 g dried fruit (rough fruit material) per day

**Limitations**:
- Aqueous extraction may yield different saponin profile than ethanol-based pharmaceutical extracts
- Talasaz 2010 case of multi-organ toxicity occurred with a traditional herbal water extract
- **Not recommended for therapeutic sexual function applications** given unpredictable content and documented toxicity risk

---

### Tinctures

**Preparation**: Fruit material extracted in 40–60% ethanol

**Typical ratio**: 1:5 (1g herb per 5mL menstruum)

**Dose range**: 2–4 mL three times daily (based on general herbal tincture conventions; no TT-specific clinical data)

**Key limitation**: No clinical trial data on tincture preparations for TT; saponin extraction in hydroalcoholic menstruum is less characterized than dry-extract standardization. Tincture dosing is empirical only.

## Administration Notes

### With vs. Without Food

- **Tribestan® clinical protocol**: After meals — reduces GI adverse effects; may enhance absorption
- **Fernández-Lázaro 2021**: Extract taken on empty stomach — reported in CrossFit athlete study; GI tolerability not specifically reported

**Recommendation**: Take with or after food to minimize nausea and GI discomfort

### Duration of Use

| Indication | Minimum duration | Trial data maximum |
|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| Erectile dysfunction | 8–12 weeks | 12 weeks (Kamenov 2017) |
| Female sexual dysfunction | 12 weeks | 120 days (De Souza 2016) |
| Male infertility | 60–90 days | 60 days (Sellandi 2012) |
| Athletic performance | 6 weeks | 6 weeks (Fernández-Lázaro 2021) |
| Hypogonadism | 3 months | 3 months (GamalEl Din 2019) |

**Long-term safety**: No controlled data beyond 4 months; exercise caution with use beyond this range

## Product Selection Guidance

### Key Selection Criteria

1. **Standardization**: Choose preparations standardized to furostanol saponins (minimum 40%, ideally ≥ 45%)
2. **Protodioscin marker**: Preparations specifying protodioscin content are preferable for sexual function and fertility applications
3. **Fruit extract**: Clinical evidence is based on fruit extract; avoid aerial-parts (herba) preparations
4. **Avoid unstandardized water extracts**: Associated with multi-organ toxicity (Talasaz 2010); unpredictable saponin content
5. **Quality assurance**: Select products from manufacturers with third-party testing (USP, NSF, or equivalent) due to documented adulteration concerns

### Red Flags in Product Labeling

- No standardization information — indicates likely variable/unreliable content
- "Testosterone booster" marketing without furostanol standardization — associated with the most severe toxicity case (Mohy-Ud-Din 2024)
- Aerial parts / herba preparations — EMA could not establish traditional-use monograph for herba; different saponin profile
