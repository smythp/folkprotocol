# Oil of Oregano - Preparations & Dosing

**Important caveat:** Human clinical dosing data for oil of oregano is limited. Only a small number of controlled human studies have been conducted. All other dosing information represents commercial practice or traditional convention, not evidence-based recommendations.

---

## Clinical Trial Doses — Whole Oregano Preparations (Human Data)

| Study | Preparation | Dose | Frequency | Duration | Indication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Force et al. 2000 | Emulsified oil of Mediterranean oregano | 600 mg/day | Not specified (likely divided) | 6 weeks | Enteric parasites |
| Nurmi et al. 2006 | Phenolic extract in juice (not essential oil) | 300 or 600 mg/day total phenolics | Daily in juice | 4 weeks | Safety/antioxidant |
| Shirvani et al. 2022 | Crude *O. vulgare* powder capsule | 500 mg | Single dose post-exercise | Acute | Exercise recovery |
| Qaraaty & Kamaneh 2020 | Oregano oil nasal spray (O. vulgare L.) | Not specified (topical intranasal) | Not specified | 4 weeks | Chronic rhinosinusitis |
| Ghitea et al. 2021 | O. vulgare essential oil | 0.2–0.6 μL concentrations | Not fully specified | 10 days | Enteric bacterial infection |

## Carvacrol-Specific Clinical Trial Doses (Primary Constituent)

*Carvacrol is the primary bioactive constituent of high-carvacrol oregano oil preparations (typically 70–86% of oil by mass). Human RCTs using purified carvacrol provide the most detailed dosing data available.*

| Study | Dose | Frequency | Duration | Indication | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ghorani et al. Phase I (2018/2021) | 1 or 2 mg/kg/day carvacrol | Daily | 1 month | Safety in healthy subjects | ~20 |
| Alavinezhad et al. 2018 | 1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol | Daily | 2 months | Asthma (Phase II) | 23 |
| Ghorani et al. 2021 | 1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol | 3× daily (divided) | 2 months | Moderate asthma (adjunct) | 33 |
| Khazdair & Boskabady 2018 | 1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol | Daily | 2 months | Sulfur mustard lung | 20 |
| Khazdair & Boskabady 2019a | 1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol | Daily | 2 months | Sulfur mustard lung | 20 |
| Khazdair & Boskabady 2019b | 1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol | Daily | 2 months | Sulfur mustard lung (chronic) | 21 |

### Carvacrol Dosing Synthesis

The convergent dose across all 5 efficacy RCTs is **1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol**. Dose equivalences for practical product selection:

| Body Weight | Carvacrol/day | Oregano oil/day at 50% carvacrol | Oregano oil/day at 75% carvacrol | Oregano oil/day at 86% carvacrol |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 kg | ~72 mg | ~144 mg | ~96 mg | ~84 mg |
| 70 kg | ~84 mg | ~168 mg | ~112 mg | ~98 mg |
| 80 kg | ~96 mg | ~192 mg | ~128 mg | ~112 mg |
| 90 kg | ~108 mg | ~216 mg | ~144 mg | ~126 mg |

**Practical summary for 70 kg adult:** 1.2 mg/kg/day carvacrol ≈ **84 mg carvacrol/day** ≈ 112–168 mg oregano oil/day depending on product carvacrol standardization.

**Safety range (Phase I):** 1–2 mg/kg/day carvacrol (≈70–140 mg/day for 70 kg adult) was safe and tolerable × 1 month. The 1.2 mg/kg/day efficacy dose falls comfortably within this range.

**Note:** Commercial oregano oil products typically range from 150–500 mg/capsule. A single 150 mg capsule standardized to 75% carvacrol delivers approximately 113 mg carvacrol — somewhat above the 84 mg target. Product-specific carvacrol content should be checked to calculate appropriate dose.

## Emulsified Oil Capsules (Most Common Commercial Form)

### Standard Commercial Dosing
- **Form:** Softgel capsules containing emulsified essential oil in carrier (olive oil common)
- **Standardization:** Typically 50–80% carvacrol per product labeling
- **Common capsule sizes:** 150 mg, 200 mg, 250 mg, 500 mg
- **Dose range:** 150–500 mg per capsule
- **Frequency:** 1–3 times daily with food
- **Total daily dose range (commercial practice):** 150–1,500 mg/day

### Evidence-Referenced Doses
- **600 mg/day** is the only human clinical dose for a therapeutic indication using whole oil (Force 2000, antiparasitic)
- **300–600 mg/day** phenolic equivalent tested for safety (Nurmi 2006); absorption confirmed, no adverse effects
- **~112–168 mg/day** oregano oil (at 50–75% carvacrol) extrapolated from carvacrol RCT dose of 1.2 mg/kg/day for a 70 kg adult; this is the best-supported efficacy dose range from human trials but has not been confirmed in studies using whole oil

### Dosing Notes
- Take with food to reduce GI irritation (heartburn, nausea)
- Emulsified form preferred over neat oil for oral bioavailability
- Start at lower end (150–300 mg/day) and titrate up based on tolerance
- Check Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for actual carvacrol percentage to calculate dose equivalence

## Liquid Oil / Drops (Oil in Carrier)

### Commercial Preparations
- **Form:** Essential oil diluted in carrier oil (typically olive oil); ratio varies by brand
- **Common dilution:** 1:4 to 1:10 (essential oil:carrier oil); roughly 10–25% essential oil
- **Dose:** 3–5 drops to 1 mL in additional carrier oil, or used sublingually with carrier
- **Frequency:** 1–3 times daily
- **Caution:** Undiluted essential oil should NEVER be consumed orally; skin/mucosa irritation risk is significant

### Preparation at Home (Diluted Oil)
- Minimum 1:4 dilution of essential oil in food-grade carrier (olive oil, MCT oil) before any oral use
- Do not use neat/undiluted essential oil orally

## Nasal Spray (Emerging Clinical Form for Rhinosinusitis)

- **Clinical precedent:** Qaraaty & Kamaneh 2020 (n=75) used an oregano oil nasal spray for CRS; exact formulation/concentration not specified in available abstract
- **No commercially established standard preparation** — this remains an investigational delivery route
- **Rationale:** Direct delivery to sinonasal mucosa; avoids systemic exposure; may achieve local antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects with lower dose
- **Status:** Single RCT; preparation details not yet characterized

## Herbal Infusion (Tea) — Whole Herb

### Standard Preparation
- **Herb:** Dried *Origanum vulgare* (aerial parts)
- **Dose:** 1–2 teaspoons (2–4 g) dried herb per 250 mL boiling water
- **Method:** Steep covered for 10–15 minutes; strain
- **Frequency:** 1–3 cups daily
- **Bioavailability note:** Aqueous infusion extracts primarily hydrophilic phenolics (rosmarinic acid, flavonoids); essential oil constituents (carvacrol, thymol) are volatile and minimally present in standard infusion — infusion and essential oil have different active compound profiles

### Indications (Traditional)
- Cough, respiratory congestion, cold symptoms
- Digestive discomfort, bloating, flatulence
- General antimicrobial support

## Tincture

### Preparation (Standard)
- **Menstruum:** 40–60% ethanol (glycerol-based tinctures also available for alcohol-sensitive individuals)
- **Herb:Liquid ratio:** 1:5 (1 g herb per 5 mL menstruum) — standard for soft/aerial herb material
- **Method:** Pack dried herb in menstruum, macerate 2–4 weeks in cool dark location; press and filter
- **Yield:** Standardized to total phenolics or carvacrol content not routine in home preparation

### Standard Tincture Dosing (Traditional / Commercial)
- **Dose:** 2–4 mL (40–80 drops) per dose
- **Frequency:** 2–3 times daily
- **Total daily:** 4–12 mL/day
- **Note:** Tincture preparations extract both hydrophilic phenolics and some lipophilic constituents depending on ethanol percentage; higher alcohol (60%+) improves essential oil extraction

### Fluid Extract (1:1 Ratio)
- **Preparation:** Concentrated; 1 mL equivalent to 1 g herb
- **Dose:** 0.5–2 mL per dose, 2–3 times daily

## Topical Applications

### Diluted Essential Oil (Skin)
- **Dilution:** 1–2% in carrier oil (1–2 drops essential oil per 5 mL carrier oil)
- **Carrier options:** Jojoba oil, fractionated coconut oil, olive oil
- **Applications:** Local antimicrobial (minor wounds, skin infections), insect bites, nail fungus
- **Caution:** Test on small skin area first; do NOT apply to face or near eyes; do NOT apply undiluted (risk of chemical burn, sensitization)
- **Maximum safe topical concentration:** EFSA notes skin/eye irritation potential; Lamiaceae allergy risk

### Steam Inhalation (Traditional)
- **Method:** 1–3 drops essential oil (or handful of fresh/dried herb) in bowl of hot water; inhale steam 5–10 minutes with towel draped over head
- **Frequency:** 1–2 times daily during acute respiratory illness
- **Note:** This delivery directly contacts respiratory mucosa with volatile carvacrol/thymol; distinct from oral dosing pharmacology

## Product Quality Selection Criteria

To choose a clinically relevant product:

1. **Species:** Must specify *Origanum vulgare* subsp. *hirtum* (or "Greek oregano," "Mediterranean oregano with high carvacrol")
2. **Carvacrol content:** ≥60% carvacrol per European Pharmacopoeia minimum; prefer 70–80% for therapeutic use
3. **Form:** Emulsified softgel preferred for oral bioavailability over liquid drops
4. **Third-party testing:** Certificate of Analysis (CoA) with GC-FID verification of carvacrol percentage
5. **Carrier:** Food-grade carrier oil (olive oil standard); avoid products with undisclosed carrier materials

## Dosing Summary by Indication

| Indication | Form | Dose | Duration | Evidence Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enteric parasites | Emulsified oil capsules | 600 mg/day | 6 weeks | Uncontrolled study (n=14) |
| Exercise recovery / antioxidant | Crude powder capsule | 500 mg single dose | Acute | Single RCT (n=24) |
| Safety confirmed range (whole preparation) | Phenolic extract | 300–600 mg/day | Up to 4 weeks | RCT (n=45) |
| Chronic rhinosinusitis | Nasal spray (oregano oil) | Not specified | 4 weeks | Single RCT (n=75); needs replication |
| Asthma / respiratory (carvacrol) | Carvacrol capsule | 1.2 mg/kg/day (~84 mg/70 kg) | 2 months | Multiple RCTs (n=23, 33, 20, 20, 21) |
| Safety range (carvacrol) | Carvacrol capsule | 1–2 mg/kg/day (~70–140 mg/70 kg) | Up to 1 month | Phase I RCT |
| Respiratory (traditional) | Infusion or steam inhalation | 1–2 tsp dried herb; 1–3 drops EO steam | Acute illness | Traditional use only |
| Digestive (traditional) | Infusion or tincture | 2–4 g herb as tea; 2–4 mL tincture 1:5 | As needed | Traditional use only |
| Topical antimicrobial | Diluted EO in carrier | 1–2% in carrier oil | As needed | Traditional use; no RCTs |
