# Devil's Claw - Preparations & Dosing

**Plant part:** Dried secondary root tubers (*Harpagophytum procumbens* and/or *H. zeyheri*)
**Active marker:** Harpagoside (≥1.2% in raw herb; ≥1.5% in standardized dry extract per European Pharmacopoeia)

---

## Dosing Principles

### Harpagoside as the Clinical Dosing Unit
Clinical evidence consistently uses **total daily harpagoside dose** as the primary dosing metric. Efficacy is harpagoside-dose-dependent (Chrubasik 1999, Gagnier 2004). Always verify harpagoside content when interpreting product labels.

### Minimum Effective Doses (Evidence-Based)
| Indication | Minimum Dose | Evidence Level |
|---|---|---|
| Acute LBP exacerbations | 50 mg harpagoside/day | Strong (Cochrane) |
| Chronic LBP exacerbations | 100 mg harpagoside/day | Moderate (single RCT) |
| Knee/hip OA | ≥30 mg harpagoside/day | Limited (above this threshold) |
| OA (stronger evidence) | 50–60 mg harpagoside/day | Moderate |

**Note:** Ethanolic extracts providing <30 mg harpagoside/day have insufficient clinical evidence (Gagnier 2004).

## Standardized Extracts (Clinical Trial Preparations)

### WS 1531 (Schwabe Pharmaceuticals)
- **Type:** Aqueous extract
- **Forms:** Tablets or capsules
- **Low dose:** 600 mg/day = 50 mg harpagoside/day
- **High dose:** 1,200 mg/day = 100 mg harpagoside/day
- **Evidence:** Used in Chrubasik 1999 dose-comparison RCT (n=183, 4 weeks); strongest evidence base for LBP
- **Dosing frequency:** Divided doses (e.g., 300 mg twice daily or 200 mg three times daily for 600 mg/day total)

### Doloteffin
- **Type:** Standardized extract
- **Dose:** Typically 2 tablets (each containing 30 mg harpagoside) = 60 mg harpagoside/day
- **Evidence:** Used in Chrubasik 2003 (vs. rofecoxib, n=88) and Chrubasik 2005 (54-week follow-up, n=73)
- **Clinical note:** Long-term 54-week safety data derived from this preparation

### Harpadol
- **Type:** Cryoground (freeze-ground) powder
- **Dose:** 6 capsules × 435 mg = 2,610 mg powder/day (containing ~50 mg harpagoside)
- **Evidence:** Used in Chantre 2000 / Leblan 2000 diacerhein comparator trials (n=122, 4 months)
- **Note:** Cryogrinding preserves iridoid content compared to heat-processed powders

### Teltonal
- **Type:** Standardized tablet
- **Dose:** 2 × 480 mg tablets/day = 960 mg/day
- **Evidence:** Used in Farpour 2021 KOA vs. meloxicam RCT (n=60, 1 month + 8-week follow-up)
- **Clinical note:** Most recent (2021) proprietary preparation with RCT evidence

## Raw Herb / Dried Root (Decoction / Tea)

### EMA Regulatory Dosing Range
- **Range:** 0.6–7.5 g dried secondary root tubers per day (EMA HMPC 2016)
- **Standard daily dose:** 1.5–4.5 g dried root for musculoskeletal pain
- **Digestive use:** Lower end of range (0.6–1.5 g/day) for digestive complaints

### Traditional Preparation (Decoction)
- **Method:** 1–2 teaspoons (4.5–9 g) dried root slices boiled in 500 mL water for 15–30 minutes; strain; drink as 1–2 cups per day
- **Challenge:** Bitter taste; may require sweetening or combination with other herbs
- **Harpagoside conversion:** ~1.2–1.5% harpagoside in compliant raw herb, so 4.5 g provides approximately 54–67 mg harpagoside — consistent with 50–60 mg clinical dosing range

### Tea / Infusion (Cold Maceration)
- **Method:** Cold maceration (overnight in cold water) may preserve iridoid content better than hot extraction, as harpagoside is sensitive to prolonged heat
- **Dose:** 1.5–2.5 g dried root in 250 mL cold water, macerated 8 hours, strained and taken in 2–3 divided doses
- **Harpagoside yield:** Lower and less predictable than standardized extracts

## Tincture / Fluid Extract

### Standard Tincture
- **Menstruum:** 25–30% ethanol (to maximize iridoid extraction; high ethanol can reduce harpagoside yield)
- **Ratio:** 1:5 (drug:extractant, by weight:volume)
- **Dose equivalent to 50 mg harpagoside:**
  - Raw herb with 1.2% harpagoside: need ~4.2 g raw herb
  - At 1:5 ratio: ~21 mL of 1:5 tincture per day
- **Practical dosing:** 5–10 mL of 1:5 tincture three times daily (15–30 mL/day total)
- **Clinical note:** Most tinctures will fall below the 50 mg harpagoside/day threshold established in high-quality trials; ethanolic extraction efficiency for harpagoside is lower than aqueous

### Fluid Extract
- **Ratio:** 1:1 or 1:2
- **Dose equivalent:** ~4 mL/day (1:1) to ~8 mL/day (1:2) for ~50 mg harpagoside
- **Challenge:** Standardization of harpagoside content is critical; "1:1 fluid extract" without harpagoside assay does not guarantee therapeutic dose

## Capsules / Tablets (Powdered Root)

### Simple Dried Root Powder
- **Dose range per trial evidence:** 2,400–2,610 mg/day
- **Harpagoside content:** Variable (0–4% in plant material); pharmacopoeial grade requires ≥1.2%
- **Typical commercial capsule:** 400–500 mg per capsule; 4–6 capsules/day to reach ~50 mg harpagoside

### Calculation for Practitioners
For any root powder or extract product:
- Required harpagoside = 50 mg/day (minimum for LBP evidence)
- Capsule harpagoside content (mg) = capsule weight (mg) × harpagoside % / 100
- Number of capsules = 50 ÷ harpagoside per capsule

**Example:** 500 mg capsule with 1.5% harpagoside = 7.5 mg harpagoside/capsule → need **~7 capsules/day** for 50 mg dose

## Duration Guidelines

| Indication | Recommended Duration | Based On |
|---|---|---|
| Acute LBP | 4 weeks | RCT evidence (Chrubasik 1996, 1999) |
| Chronic LBP / recurrent | Up to 54 weeks (with monitoring) | Chrubasik 2005 open-label |
| Osteoarthritis | 4 months minimum | Chantre 2000, Leblan 2000 |
| Digestive complaints | 2–4 weeks (traditional use indication) | EMA traditional classification |

---

## Proprietary Products Not in Clinical Trials

**Warning from Bargsten & Seifert 2025:** 88 products analyzed; wide heterogeneity in ADR reporting:
- Only 6% of food supplements report adverse drug reaction information
- 100% of dietary supplements report no ADR information
- Significant gaps in consumer information for non-pharmaceutical-grade products

**Recommendation:** Prefer products that:
1. State harpagoside content per serving (not just "Harpagophytum extract")
2. Are manufactured to pharmaceutical-grade standards or equivalent GMP
3. Disclose the drug-extract ratio and extraction solvent
