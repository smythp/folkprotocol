# Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia) - Safety Profile

## Overall Safety Assessment

**Summary:** Safety profile is MIXED. Clinical trials report good tolerability, but the EFSA rejection on genotoxicity grounds and the widespread adulteration problem create significant real-world risk that goes beyond what trial data alone would suggest.

## EFSA 2021 Rejection — Genotoxicity Concern

**Source:** European Food Safety Authority, "Safety of Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia) root extract as a novel food pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2015/2283" (2021)
**URL:** https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/6936

**Proposed use evaluated:** Up to 200 mg/day for adult population, excluding pregnant and lactating women

**EFSA Findings:**
- **"The Panel concluded that the safety of the novel food has not been established."**
- The extract is **clastogenic in vitro** (causes chromosomal breakage in cell culture)
- In an **in vivo mammalian alkaline comet assay**, the extract **induced DNA damage in the stomach and duodenum**
- **Conclusion: "Safety not established under any condition of use."**

**What this means:**
- EFSA did not approve tongkat ali as a novel food in the EU
- The genotoxicity findings are concerning because they suggest potential for DNA damage at the site of first contact (GI tract)
- "Clastogenic" means causing chromosome breaks — this is a more serious genotoxicity finding than simple point mutations
- The in vivo DNA damage (stomach, duodenum) is particularly concerning because it demonstrates the effect isn't limited to cell culture conditions
- This does NOT mean tongkat ali definitely causes cancer, but it means the safety data was insufficient to rule out genotoxic risk

**Context:** Clinical trials (4–24 weeks duration) have not reported increased cancer incidence, but these are far too short and small to detect carcinogenic effects. The EFSA rejection is based on mechanistic toxicology, not epidemiological evidence.

## Adulteration — The Biggest Real-World Safety Risk

**This is the most critical safety issue for tongkat ali in practice.**

### Pharmaceutical Adulterants
- Many tongkat ali products have been found to contain **undeclared prescription drugs**, specifically:
  - **Sildenafil** (Viagra) — a PDE5 inhibitor with serious drug interactions and contraindications
  - **Tadalafil** (Cialis) — another PDE5 inhibitor
- These are added intentionally to create perceived "efficacy" for sexual enhancement claims
- Products spiked with PDE5 inhibitors are dangerous for:
  - Anyone taking nitrates (potentially fatal hypotension)
  - People with cardiovascular disease
  - Those on alpha-blockers or other antihypertensives
  - Anyone who doesn't know they're taking a prescription drug

### Heavy Metal Contamination
- **Mercury** contamination documented in tongkat ali products
- **Lead** contamination documented
- These are particularly concerning with long-term use

### Product Quality Failures
- **Many products found to contain little to no actual Eurycoma longifolia**
- Ratio extract claims (100:1, 200:1) are unverifiable and often fraudulent
- The combination of high demand, expensive raw material (slow-growing tropical tree), and sexual enhancement marketing creates strong economic incentives for adulteration

### Recommendation
- Use only products from manufacturers with third-party testing and Certificate of Analysis (CoA)
- Standardization to a specific eurycomanone percentage (e.g., 0.8–1.5%) is a minimum quality indicator
- Products making aggressive sexual enhancement claims are more likely to be adulterated
- Consider products compliant with Malaysian Standard MS 2409:2011

## Drug Interactions

### CYP3A4 Inhibition
- Eurycoma longifolia may inhibit CYP3A4, a major drug-metabolizing enzyme
- CYP3A4 metabolizes approximately 50% of all pharmaceutical drugs
- Inhibition could increase blood levels of CYP3A4 substrates, potentially causing toxicity
- **Affected drug classes include:** statins (atorvastatin, simvastatin), calcium channel blockers, many antibiotics, immunosuppressants (cyclosporine, tacrolimus), benzodiazepines, and many others

### Antidiabetic Drugs
- May enhance blood sugar lowering effects
- Specific interaction noted with metformin
- Risk: hypoglycemia if combined without monitoring

### Antihypertensive Drugs
- May interfere with propranolol (beta-blocker)
- Combined hypotensive effects possible

### Immunosuppressants
- Potential to interfere with immunosuppressive therapy
- Mechanism not well characterized
- Particularly concerning for transplant patients

### Anticoagulants / Antiplatelets
- May increase bleeding risk
- Caution with warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel, and similar drugs

## Contraindications

### Absolute Contraindications
- **Pregnant women** — hormonal effects, genotoxicity concern
- **Breastfeeding women** — insufficient safety data, hormonal effects
- **Children** — no pediatric safety data, hormonal activity inappropriate

### Relative Contraindications (Use Only Under Medical Supervision)
- **Hormone-sensitive cancers** — prostate cancer, breast cancer, endometrial cancer; any hormonal modulation is contraindicated
- **Heart disease** — potential cardiovascular effects, drug interactions
- **Liver disease** — insufficient hepatic safety data, potential for drug interaction via CYP inhibition
- **Kidney disease** — insufficient renal safety data
- **Weakened immune systems** — potential immunomodulatory effects, interaction risk with immunosuppressants

## Adverse Effects from Clinical Trials

### Commonly Reported (Mild)
- Insomnia
- Irritability
- Restlessness
- Gastrointestinal upset

### Trial Safety Data
- Leisegang 2022 meta-analysis (9 RCTs, 799 males): "No significant differences in adverse events compared to placebo"
- Leitao 2021 (105 men, 12 weeks): "Supplementation was well-tolerated"
- Talbott 2013 (63 subjects, 4 weeks): No serious adverse events reported

**Important caveat:** Trial durations (4–24 weeks) and sample sizes are insufficient to detect rare or long-term adverse effects. The EFSA genotoxicity findings suggest potential long-term risks that short trials cannot capture.
