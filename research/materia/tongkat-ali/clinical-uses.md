# Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia) - Clinical Uses

## Testosterone & Hormonal Effects

### Meta-Analysis: Testosterone in Men (Overall)
**Study:** Leisegang et al. 2022 (systematic review and meta-analysis)
**Source:** Medicina (Kaunas), PMID: 36013514
**Scope:** 9 RCTs, 799 males
**Dosing across studies:** 100–600 mg/day for 1 day to 24 weeks

**Results:**
- Total testosterone: significantly increased (MD 1.59 nmol/L; p = 0.02)
- **Subgroup — hypogonadal men:** significant increase (MD 2.27 nmol/L; p = 0.02)
- **Subgroup — healthy eugonadal men:** NO significant effect (p = 0.50)
- Significant increases observed only with supplementation durations > 8 weeks
- No significant differences in adverse events vs placebo

**Critical interpretation:** The testosterone-raising effect appears LIMITED TO MEN WITH LOW TESTOSTERONE. Healthy men with normal testosterone levels showed no significant change. This is the single most important clinical finding — tongkat ali appears to normalize, not elevate beyond baseline.

### Dose Comparison: 100 mg vs 200 mg in Hypogonadal Men
**Study:** Leitao et al. 2021 (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter)
**Source:** Andrologia, PMID: 33543660
**Sample:** 105 men, aged 50–70 years, with low testosterone (<300 ng/dL)
**Duration:** 12 weeks
**Extract:** Physta standardized Eurycoma longifolia root extract
**Funding:** Biotropics Malaysia (product manufacturer)

**Arms:**
- 100 mg/day Physta
- 200 mg/day Physta
- Placebo

**Testosterone Results:**
- 100 mg group: Total T increased from 8.85 → 10.11 nmol/L
- 200 mg group: Total T increased from 9.30 → 11.23 nmol/L
- Placebo group: Total T decreased from 9.47 → 9.22 nmol/L
- 200 mg group showed significant increase vs placebo at weeks 4, 8, and 12

**Secondary Outcomes:**
- Ageing Male Symptoms (AMS) scores: significantly reduced in both Physta groups
- Fatigue Severity Scale (FSS) scores: significantly reduced in both Physta groups
- Cortisol: significantly decreased in the 200 mg group
- Muscle strength (handgrip): significantly increased in both Physta groups

**Clinical significance:** 200 mg/day appears to be the more effective dose. The 100 mg dose produced smaller testosterone increases. Both doses improved subjective symptom scores. Note manufacturer funding — all Physta studies should be read with this conflict in mind.

## Stress & Cortisol

### Cortisol Reduction and Mood in Moderately Stressed Subjects
**Study:** Talbott et al. 2013 (placebo-controlled trial)
**Source:** Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, PMID: 23705671
**Sample:** 63 subjects (32 men, 31 women) with moderate stress
**Dosing:** 200 mg/day standardized hot-water extract (Physta)
**Duration:** 4 weeks

**Results:**
- Cortisol exposure: **−16%** in tongkat ali group vs placebo (p < 0.05)
- Testosterone levels: **+37%** in tongkat ali group vs placebo (p < 0.05)
- Tension: −11% (p < 0.05)
- Anger: −12% (p < 0.05)
- Confusion: −15% (p < 0.05)

**Clinical significance:** This is the key stress/cortisol study. The cortisol-lowering effect is notable because cortisol suppresses testosterone production — the testosterone increase here may be secondary to cortisol reduction rather than a direct androgenic effect. This is consistent with the meta-analysis finding that eugonadal men don't benefit (their cortisol/testosterone axis isn't dysregulated).

## Erectile Function

### Meta-Analysis: Erectile Function (IIEF-5)
**Study:** Kotirum et al. 2015 (systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs)
**Source:** Complementary Therapies in Medicine, PMID: 26527235
**Scope:** 2 RCTs, 139 subjects

**Results:**
- Overall IIEF-5 score change: NOT significant (MD 1.34, p = 0.45)
- **Subgroup — subjects with lower baseline IIEF-5 scores:** significant improvement (MD 3.32, p = 0.04)
- No serious adverse side effects reported

**Clinical significance:** Similar pattern to testosterone — benefits only in those with existing dysfunction. Healthy erectile function is not enhanced. Limited by very small evidence base (only 2 RCTs available).

### Sexual Performance and Well-Being (Combination Product)
**Study:** Udani et al. 2014 (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled)
**Source:** Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine, PMID: 24386427
**Sample:** 26 men, aged 40–65 years
**Dosing:** 300 mg/day total (Physta 200 mg + Polygonum minus 100 mg)
**Duration:** 12 weeks

**Results:**
- Erection hardness scale: significant improvement (p = 0.038)
- Sexual Health Inventory for Men (SHIM): significant improvement (p = 0.005)
- Testosterone levels: NO significant changes

**Limitations:** Very small sample (n=26). Combination product — impossible to attribute effects to tongkat ali alone. The lack of testosterone change alongside improved sexual function suggests a mechanism independent of testosterone elevation.

## Pattern Across Studies

The consistent finding across meta-analyses and RCTs is that tongkat ali appears to **normalize dysregulated systems** rather than enhance already-normal function:
- Testosterone increases: only in hypogonadal men (Leisegang 2022)
- Erectile function improvements: only in those with lower baseline scores (Kotirum 2015)
- Cortisol reduction and mood improvements: only in moderately stressed subjects (Talbott 2013)

This "normalizing" pattern is important for setting realistic expectations and identifying appropriate candidates for supplementation.
