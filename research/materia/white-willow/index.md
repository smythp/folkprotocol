## Evidence Snapshot

### Strongest Finding
Chrubasik 2000 (n=210): **39% pain-free** at 4 weeks with 240 mg salicin vs **6% placebo** (p<0.001)

### Active Comparator
Chrubasik 2001 (n=228): Willow bark **equivalent to rofecoxib** (COX-2 inhibitor) at ~40% lower cost

### Long-term Safety
Uehleke 2013 (n=436): 45.6% pain improvement at 6 months with 0 significant adverse events

### Meta-analysis
Lin 2023 (6 RCTs, n=329): Pain SMD −0.31 (p=0.007); Physical function WOMAC SMD −0.80

---

## Key Mechanistic Insight

At 240 mg salicin/day, serum salicylate equivalent is only ~87 mg aspirin — far below analgesic doses. Polyphenols and flavonoids are primary co-contributors to anti-inflammatory effect. This explains:
- Lower GI damage than aspirin/diclofenac
- Minimal antiplatelet activity (unlike aspirin)
- Clinical efficacy despite sub-analgesic salicylate levels
