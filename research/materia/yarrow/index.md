## Quick Reference

### Clinical Evidence
- **6 RCTs:** MS (n=75, 1yr), T2D (n=60, 90d), dysmenorrhea (teabag, 2 cycles), oral mucositis (n=56, 14d), IBS mixture (n=60), hemorrhoids (n=54, 10d)
- **1 systematic review:** 6 trials; dysmenorrhea/menorrhagia — effective and safe conclusion
- **Highest-quality trial:** Triple-blind MS RCT (Ayoobi 2019)

### Key Doses

| Indication | Dose | Source |
|-----------|------|--------|
| GI / appetite (regulatory) | 2–4 g dried herb × 3/day (tea) | Commission E 1990 |
| MS (add-on) | 250–500 mg/day aqueous extract × 1yr | Ayoobi 2019 |
| T2D (liver/lipids) | 500 mg/day capsule × 90d | Daneshvar-Ghahfarokhi 2025 |
| Dysmenorrhea | Teabag × 3 days/cycle × 2 cycles | Jenabi 2015 |
| Hemorrhoids (topical) | 5% ointment × 3×/day × 10d | Mahmoudi 2023 |
| Oral mucositis (mouthwash) | 15 mL distillate × 4×/day × 14d | Miranzadeh 2015 |

### Critical Safety

- **Contact dermatitis** from sesquiterpene lactones (alpha-peroxyachifolid) — most documented adverse effect
- **Digoxin:** quercetin inhibits P-gp → **413% ↑ AUC** — major DDI
- **CYP450 inhibition** by chamazulene (1A2, 2C9, 2D6, 3A4) — warfarin, cyclosporine risk
- **Pregnancy:** contraindicated — emmenagogue/uterotonic
- **Asteraceae allergy:** contraindicated
- **Essential oil (concentrated):** camphor and thujone toxicity — not internal use

### Regulatory Status
- Commission E (1990): **positive monograph** — appetite, dyspeptic ailments
- EMA/HMPC flos (2011): traditional use — GI complaints, menstrual spasm, superficial wounds
- EMA/HMPC herba (Rev.1): same indications
- European Pharmacopoeia: official quality standards (herba: ≥2 mL/kg EO; flos: ≥0.02% chamazulene)
- ESCOP: published monograph
- WHO: no monograph identified
