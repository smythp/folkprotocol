# Yarrow — Clinical Uses

*Achillea millefolium L.*

## Overview

Six human RCTs have been conducted across diverse indications. Methodological quality is moderate; sample sizes are small (n=54–75) and standardization disclosure is frequently inadequate. The multiple sclerosis trial is the highest-quality study to date (triple-blind, 1 year, 3 parallel arms).

---

## 1. Multiple Sclerosis (Relapsing-Remitting)

**Source:** Ayoobi et al. 2019 — PMID 30599916
**Design:** Triple-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-group
**Population:** 75 patients with relapsing-remitting MS
**Intervention:** Aqueous extract of *A. millefolium* (3 groups)

| Group | Dose | Duration |
|-------|------|----------|
| Placebo | — | 1 year |
| Low dose | 250 mg/day | 1 year |
| High dose | 500 mg/day | 1 year |

**Key outcomes (both active doses vs. placebo):**
- Significantly reduced annual relapse rate
- Increased time to first relapse
- Improved MSFC (Multiple Sclerosis Functional Composite) z-scores
- Decreased EDSS (Expanded Disability Status Scale) scores
- Cognitive improvement: word-pair learning, PASAT, WCST

**500 mg/day additionally:**
- Significantly reduced lesion volume changes on MRI

**No serious adverse events reported.**

**Limitations:** Add-on therapy (all patients received standard DMT); cannot isolate direct neurological vs. anti-inflammatory mechanism; aqueous extract not standardized; 75 patients across 3 groups limits power.

## 2. Type 2 Diabetes — Liver Enzymes & Lipid Profile

**Source:** Daneshvar-Ghahfarokhi et al. 2025 — PMID 39856724
**Design:** Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled
**Population:** 60 adults with T2D (30 intervention, 30 control)
**Dose:** 500 mg/day encapsulated dried *A. millefolium* herb
**Duration:** 90 days

**Results (intervention vs. placebo):**

| Marker | Baseline | 90 Days | p-value |
|--------|----------|---------|---------|
| ALT (U/L) | 28.79 | 24.41 | 0.017 |
| AST (U/L) | 24.28 | 18.76 | 0.007 |
| Triglycerides (mg/dL) | 161.7 | 147.3 | 0.045 |
| LDL (mg/dL) | 98.90 | 80.86 | 0.001 |
| Total cholesterol (mg/dL) | 168.3 | 150.1 | 0.006 |
| HDL | NS | NS | — |
| FBS / HbA1c | NS | NS | — |

**Key finding:** Hepatoprotective and lipid-lowering effects confirmed; glycemic outcomes unaffected.
**Limitations:** Small sample; no standardization data on capsule; 3-month follow-up only.

## 3. Primary Dysmenorrhea

**Source:** Jenabi & Fereidoony 2015 — PMID 26238568
**Design:** Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled
**Population:** Female university students with primary dysmenorrhea
**Intervention:** *A. millefolium* teabag vs. placebo teabag, 3 days/cycle
**Duration:** 2 menstrual cycles

**Results (VAS pain score change):**
- Cycle 1: significant improvement vs. placebo (P=0.001)
- Cycle 2: significant improvement vs. placebo (P<0.0001)
- Progressive benefit across cycles suggests cumulative effect

**No adverse events reported.**

**Systematic review support:** Moradi et al. 2024 — systematic review of 6 trials (3 dysmenorrhea, 2 menorrhagia) concluded yarrow is "effective and safe" for primary dysmenorrhea and menstrual bleeding reduction.

**Limitations:** Sample size not specified in publication abstract; teabag form not dose-standardized; 2-cycle window limits conclusions.

## 4. Chemotherapy-Induced Oral Mucositis

**Source:** Miranzadeh et al. 2015 — PMID 25667123
**Design:** Double-blind, randomized controlled trial
**Population:** 56 cancer patients with chemotherapy-induced oral mucositis
**Intervention:** 15 mL of mixture (routine solution + distilled *A. millefolium*), gargled 4×/day
**Duration:** 14 days

**Results:**

| Group | Baseline OM severity | Day 14 | Change |
|-------|---------------------|--------|--------|
| Experimental (n≈28) | 2.39 ± 0.875 | 0.32 ± 0.54 | −2.07 ↓ (p<0.001) |
| Control (n≈28) | 2.89 ± 0.956 | 2.89 (worsened) | NS or worse |

**No adverse events from herbal preparation reported.**

**Limitations:** Small sample; single-center; heterogeneous cancer/chemo types; distillate preparation not standardized; combined with routine solution (yarrow contribution not isolated).

## 5. Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)

**Source:** Kazemian et al. 2017 — PMID 29259631
**Design:** Randomized controlled clinical trial
**Population:** 60 IBS patients with mild-to-moderate symptoms (mean age 38.75 ± 11.74 years)
**Intervention:** Multi-herb mixture — *Boswellia carterii* + *Zingiber officinale* + *Achillea millefolium* vs. placebo

**Results (intervention vs. placebo):**
- Abdominal pain severity and frequency: significantly reduced (P<0.05)
- Bloating: significantly reduced
- Depression scores: significantly reduced
- Anxiety scores: significantly reduced
- Quality of life: significant improvement in men (P=0.01); not significant in women

**Critical limitation:** Multi-herb preparation — yarrow-specific contribution cannot be quantified. Dose per individual herb not reported.

---

## 6. Hemorrhoids (Grade 1–2)

**Source:** Mahmoudi et al. 2023 — Journal of Herbal Medicine (not PubMed indexed)
**Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
**Population:** 54 patients with grade 1–2 internal hemorrhoids
**Intervention:** 5% hydroalcoholic extract *A. millefolium* ointment, applied 3×/day
**Duration:** 10 days

**Results:**

| Symptom | Outcome |
|---------|---------|
| Pain | Significantly reduced (p<0.05) |
| Discomfort during defecation | Significantly improved |
| Bleeding (intensity + frequency) | Significantly reduced |
| Itching | No significant improvement vs. placebo |

**Limitations:** Short duration (10 days); grade 1–2 only; not PubMed-indexed; no follow-up data.

|-----------|-------------|----------------|
